WORKING PRINCIPLE

The Automatic Night Lamp operates based on the intensity of surrounding light.

1. The LDR senses the surrounding light intensity.
2. Its resistance changes according to the light level.
3. The sensing circuit produces a control signal.
4. The transistor acts as an electronic switch.
5. When the surrounding light decreases, the LED turns ON.
6. When sufficient light is available, the LED turns OFF.
7. The potentiometer adjusts the sensitivity of the circuit.

Operating Conditions

- Daytime: LED OFF.
- Nighttime: LED ON.

The switching threshold can be adjusted using the potentiometer.
